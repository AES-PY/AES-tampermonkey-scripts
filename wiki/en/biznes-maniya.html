Business Mania is a multiplayer online economic game, business simulator, and business training game. The main objective is to develop your own business in any of the available fields: manufacturing, trade, mining, construction, advertising, and others.

Players earn virtual money by producing and selling products and services. Success in the game depends on choosing the right strategy: what and where to produce, which goods are best to sell, and which markets will have the highest demand.

In the game, players compete and cooperate with each other. Players' businesses are closely interconnected: manufacturers depend on raw material suppliers, traders on product suppliers, and so on. The instability of the market economy and fluctuations in supply and demand force players to seek long-term partners. It is this atmosphere of competition and cooperation that makes the game so exciting!

Business Mania is an entertaining project. The main goal of the game is to give its players positive emotions, engage them in the gameplay, and encourage communication. But the game also has another very important component: training. Through a game, participants master the basics of financial management, learn to understand accounting reports, and grasp basic economic concepts.
